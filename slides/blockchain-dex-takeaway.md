Make sure to get the following takeaway:

### Advantages of decentralized exchanges:
- No counterparty risk
- Keep control off your token until the change

### Problem with decentralized exchanges:
- Order books (the dominant exchange type in traditional finance) are difficult to implement on chain, due to gas fees.
- Off chain matching can implement order book exchanges. Requires trust that matching is done correctly.
